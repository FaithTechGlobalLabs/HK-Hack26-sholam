# FaithMap — 132 Curated Discover Questions

**Audience:** High-school students and young people (approximately ages 13–20).

**Editorial standard:** Keep only questions with a clear curiosity hook, a recognizable connection to real life, a surprising biblical detail, or a meaningful question worth exploring. Avoid generic moral reminders, school/parent-style advice, and questions that sound like a worksheet.

**Important:** Bible references are starting points for investigation, not automatic proof of a predetermined answer. Lessons should distinguish what the biblical text explicitly says, historical evidence, Christian interpretation, and areas where Christians disagree.
